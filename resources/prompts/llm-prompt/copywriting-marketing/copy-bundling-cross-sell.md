prompt_preview: |-
  You are an expert in eCommerce upselling and cross-selling.
  My customer is about to buy [INSERT MAIN PRODUCT], but I want to convince them to upgrade to a "Bundling Package" that includes [INSERT ADDITIONAL PRODUCT].

  Please write a short, highly persuasive Upsell/Cross-sell Copy. 
  Structure it as follows:
  1. **The Compliment:** Validate their excellent choice on the main product.
  2. **The Missing Piece:** Explain why their experience won't be 100% complete without the additional product (Create a small gap in logic).
  3. **The No-Brainer Offer:** Present the bundled price as an absolute steal (e.g., "Add it now for only $X instead of $Y").
  4. **Urgency:** Explain that this bundle is a one-time offer.

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Teks *Upsell* yang logis dan manipulatif secara halus:
- Menyentuh sisi rasional pembeli dengan membuktikan bahwa paket *Bundling* jauh lebih hemat (No-Brainer Offer).
- Menciptakan efek FOMO dengan menyatakan penawaran tersebut akan hangus begitu mereka menutup halaman.
