  Act as a Senior Media Buyer and Direct-Response Copywriter.
  I need a high-converting ad copy for a Facebook/Instagram Ads campaign.

  Product Details:
  - Product Name: [INSERT PRODUCT]
  - Target Audience: [INSERT AUDIENCE]
  - Pain Point solved: [INSERT PAIN POINT]
  - Offer/Promo: [INSERT OFFER, e.g., 20% off, Free Shipping]

  Please write the ad copy using the AIDA framework:
  1. **Attention:** A strong opening hook that calls out the target audience's pain point.
  2. **Interest:** Present the product as the exact solution they've been looking for.
  3. **Desire:** Paint a picture of what their life will look like after using the product (Highlight benefits, not just features).
  4. **Action:** A clear, frictionless Call-to-Action (CTA) telling them exactly what to click.

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Sebuah naskah iklan (*Ad Copy*) yang mengalir mulus:
- **Attention (Perhatian):** Kalimat pembuka yang memfilter audiens (Misal: "Khusus untuk pria yang mulai mengalami kebotakan...").
- **Interest & Desire (Minat & Hasrat):** Penjelasan rasional sekaligus emosional kenapa produk Anda adalah jalan keluar terbaik.
- **Action (Tindakan):** Perintah psikologis yang mengarahkan jempol mereka ke tombol klik.
