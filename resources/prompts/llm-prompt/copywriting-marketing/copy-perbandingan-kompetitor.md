prompt_preview: |-
  You are an expert in Brand Positioning and Copywriting.
  I want to write a comparison copy (for a landing page or social media post) showing why my product is better than the mainstream competitors. 
  However, I want to sound professional and objective, not arrogant or petty.

  - My Product: [INSERT YOUR PRODUCT]
  - My Key Strengths: [INSERT WHAT YOU DO BETTER]
  - The Competitor(s): [INSERT COMPETITOR NAME OR "Traditional Alternatives"]
  - The Competitor's Weakness: [INSERT WHAT THEY LACK]

  Please write a persuasive "Us vs. Them" comparison narrative. 
  Frame the competitor as "good for the old days", but frame my product as the "modern, superior solution for today's needs".
steps:
  - "Salin prompt di atas."
  - "Buka Claude (Claude sangat jago merangkai kata-kata diplomatis namun tetap menusuk *pain point* kompetitor)."
  - "Tulis keunggulan produk Anda dan kelemahan terbesar kompetitor Anda."
  - "Tekan *enter*."
tips:
  - "Daripada menyebut nama *brand* kompetitor secara gamblang (yang bisa memicu masalah hukum), gunakan julukan generik seperti 'Produk Skincare Lama Anda' atau 'Cara Tradisional'."

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Narasi *Positioning* "Us vs Them" yang cerdas:
- **Diplomatis:** AI akan mengakui kelebihan metode lama/kompetitor, lalu menghancurkannya secara logis dengan solusi modern Anda.
- Mengedukasi pelanggan untuk mengerti alasan di balik mahalnya/uniknya produk Anda dibandingkan yang ada di pasaran.
