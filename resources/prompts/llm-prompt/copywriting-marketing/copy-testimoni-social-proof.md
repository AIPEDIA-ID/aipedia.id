prompt_preview: |-
  You are an expert copywriter specializing in "Social Proof" advertising.
  I want to run a Facebook/Instagram Ad using a real customer testimonial as the main hook.

  Here is the raw review from my customer:
  "[INSERT RAW CUSTOMER REVIEW HERE]"
  
  Product being sold: [INSERT PRODUCT NAME]

  Please transform this review into a highly persuasive Ad Copy. 
  Structure:
  1. Put the most dramatic or impressive sentence from the review in quotes at the very top as the Headline.
  2. Elaborate slightly on the customer's journey (the problem they had vs the result they got).
  3. Introduce the product seamlessly as the hero.
  4. End with a Call-to-Action to buy/learn more.

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Teks iklan yang bertumpu pada validasi publik (Social Proof):
- **Kutipan (Quotes) di awal paragraf:** Memamerkan pujian terbaik si pembeli sebagai judul utama yang membius (*Headline*).
- **Elaborasi Empati:** AI akan merapikan tulisan pelanggan yang berantakan menjadi cerita keberhasilan (*Success Story*) yang terstruktur, meyakinkan pembaca untuk mengklik tombol beli.
