prompt_preview: |-
  You are an elite copywriter specializing in the PAS framework.
  I need a highly persuasive sales copy for a landing page / social media post.

  My Product: [INSERT PRODUCT]
  Target Audience: [INSERT AUDIENCE]
  The Core Problem: [INSERT THEIR PROBLEM]

  Please write a compelling piece of copy strictly following the PAS formula:
  1. **Problem:** Call out the specific pain point immediately. Make them feel understood.
  2. **Agitate:** Twist the knife. Explain how ignoring this problem makes their life worse, costs them money, or hurts their ego.
  3. **Solve:** Introduce my product as the inevitable, perfect solution that makes the pain go away instantly.

  - "Tekan *enter*."
tips:
  - "Formula PAS sangat luar biasa kuat untuk produk kesehatan, asuransi, atau *software* B2B yang menyelesaikan masalah spesifik."

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Eksploitasi masalah pelanggan (*Pain Point*):
- **Problem (Masalah):** Kalimat pembuka yang membuat pelanggan berkata *"Wah, ini gue banget!"*.
- **Agitate (Mengaduk-aduk):** Menjelaskan skenario terburuk jika mereka tidak mau menyelesaikan masalah tersebut sekarang.
- **Solve (Solusi):** Produk Anda hadir bersinar terang sebagai penyelamat.
