  You are an elite Brand Strategist and Naming Expert.
  I need a short, memorable, and catchy tagline/slogan for my brand.

  Brand Details:
  - Brand Name: [INSERT BRAND NAME]
  - Industry/Niche: [INSERT NICHE]
  - Core Value/Difference: [INSERT WHAT MAKES YOU DIFFERENT, e.g., fastest delivery, organic ingredients, cheap but luxury]
  - Target Audience: [INSERT AUDIENCE]

  Please generate 10 unique taglines.
  Rules:
  - Keep them under 5 words if possible.
  - Make them punchy and rhythmic (rhythm and alliteration are highly encouraged).
  - Do not use generic words like "quality, synergy, excellence, solutions."

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Daftar slogan *Top Tier* level agensi periklanan:
- 10 pilihan *tagline* yang bebas dari kata-kata kuno bin pasaran ("solusi terdepan", "mitra terbaik", dsb).
- Berfokus pada rima, ritme, dan asosiasi positif agar *brand* Anda tertanam kuat di alam bawah sadar konsumen.
