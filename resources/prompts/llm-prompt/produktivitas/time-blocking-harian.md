  Act as a peak-performance productivity coach. 
  I want to create a daily "Time Blocking" schedule based on my tasks and my natural energy levels throughout the day.

  My Parameters:
  - Working Hours: [INSERT WORKING HOURS, e.g., 09:00 to 17:00]
  - My High-Energy/Focus Time: [INSERT HIGH ENERGY HOURS, e.g., Morning 09:00-11:00]
  - My Low-Energy Time: [INSERT LOW ENERGY HOURS, e.g., After lunch 13:00-15:00]
  
  My Tasks for Today:
  [INSERT TASKS]

  Please create a time-blocked schedule. Place highly demanding, creative, or analytical tasks during my High-Energy time, and routine, admin, or easy tasks during my Low-Energy time. Don't forget to include short breaks!

  - "Tuliskan daftar tugas Anda hari ini, lalu tekan *enter*."

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

AI tidak hanya membuat *to-do list* biasa, melainkan **Jadwal Jam per Jam** yang cerdas:
- **Tugas Berat (Deep Work):** Otomatis dialokasikan di jam-jam Anda merasa paling segar.
- **Tugas Ringan (Shallow Work):** Membalas email atau hal administratif akan diletakkan di jam-jam Anda sedang kehabisan energi (misal: setelah makan siang).
- **Jeda (Breaks):** Rekomendasi waktu istirahat yang proporsional.
