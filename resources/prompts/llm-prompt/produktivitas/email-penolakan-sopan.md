  You are an expert in business communication and diplomacy.
  I need to decline an invitation, request, or proposal from someone, but it is extremely important that I do not burn bridges or offend them.

  Context:
  - Sender: [INSERT SENDER NAME/RELATIONSHIP]
  - What they asked for: [INSERT WHAT THEY REQUESTED]
  - Real reason for declining: [INSERT YOUR REAL REASON, e.g., too busy, no budget, not interested]

  Please write a highly polite, warm, and professional email declining the request. Frame the rejection gracefully (e.g., pivot to a "not right now" or soften the blow). Give me 2 variations:
  1. A soft rejection (leaving the door open for the future).
  2. A firm but polite rejection (closing the matter completely).

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

AI akan memberikan Anda **dua opsi email penolakan**:
- **Opsi 1 (Pintu Terbuka):** Menolak dengan alasan waktu/situasi, tapi memberi sinyal bahwa Anda bersedia berdiskusi lagi di lain waktu (bulan depan/tahun depan).
- **Opsi 2 (Tegas & Final):** Menolak dengan sangat ramah namun menutup kemungkinan adanya negosiasi lanjutan (cocok untuk proposal *spam* atau *vendor* yang gigih).
