  Act as an expert HR consultant and career coach.
  I need to write my self-assessment / performance review for this period, and I want to sound confident, impactful, and professional without sounding arrogant.

  Here is my rough data:
  - My Role: [INSERT JOB TITLE]
  - Major Accomplishments: [INSERT ACCOMPLISHMENTS, e.g., launched project X, increased sales by 10%]
  - Areas I struggled with / Areas for growth: [INSERT STRUGGLES, e.g., time management, learning new software]
  - Goals for next period: [INSERT NEXT GOALS]

  Please draft a comprehensive self-assessment narrative based on the above. Structure it elegantly, turning my raw accomplishments into strong "impact statements" and framing my struggles as proactive "learning opportunities."

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Narasi evaluasi diri yang sangat kuat:
- **Impact Statements:** Capaian Anda akan ditulis ulang menggunakan kata kerja aktif (action verbs) yang berfokus pada hasil/dampak bagi perusahaan.
- **Growth Areas:** Kelemahan Anda akan diubah narasinya menjadi "Ruang untuk bertumbuh" atau "Inisiatif perbaikan diri".
- **Future Goals:** Terlihat ambisius namun terukur untuk periode kerja berikutnya.
