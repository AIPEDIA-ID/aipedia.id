  You are an expert in professional communication and workplace diplomacy. 
  I need to send a follow-up/reminder message to a colleague regarding a pending task or missing information. 
  
  Please draft a polite, professional, but firm message that clearly communicates urgency without sounding aggressive or accusatory.

  Details:
  - Colleague's Name: [INSERT COLLEAGUE NAME]
  - What I am waiting for: [INSERT WHAT YOU NEED, e.g., the Q3 financial report]
  - Original deadline (if any): [INSERT PAST DEADLINE OR 'None']
  - Why it is urgent/impact: [INSERT WHY YOU NEED IT NOW, e.g., we cannot proceed with the client pitch without it]

  Provide two variations of the message:
  1. A formal version (suitable for email).
  2. A casual but firm version (suitable for Slack/WhatsApp).

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

AI akan memberikan Anda **dua pilihan draf pesan**:
1. **Versi Formal (Email):** Struktur pesan yang sangat rapi, sopan, dan jelas, cocok untuk komunikasi resmi antar departemen.
2. **Versi Kasual (Chat/WhatsApp):** Pesan yang lebih rileks, pendek, namun tetap menjaga profesionalisme dan *urgency* untuk segera ditindaklanjuti.
