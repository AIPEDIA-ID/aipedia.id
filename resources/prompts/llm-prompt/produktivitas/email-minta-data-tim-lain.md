  You are a highly diplomatic and effective corporate communicator.
  I need to write an email to another department/team to request specific data or information. Often, cross-department requests get ignored or delayed, so the tone must be polite, appreciative, and highlight mutual benefit.

  Context:
  - Recipient's Name/Department: [INSERT NAME OR DEPARTMENT]
  - What data I need: [INSERT EXACT DATA/INFO NEEDED]
  - Why I need it (Business Context): [INSERT WHY YOU NEED IT]
  - Deadline (if any): [INSERT DEADLINE]

  Please draft a short, professional email that clearly explains the request, shows respect for their time, and explicitly states the business reason (the "Why") so they are motivated to help.

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Draf email profesional yang mencakup:
- **Subjek Email Jelas:** Tidak membingungkan saat masuk *inbox*.
- **Pembuka Empatik:** Menghargai kesibukan mereka.
- **Inti Permintaan:** Detail data yang diminta secara poin-per-poin.
- **Alasan Bisnis:** Menjelaskan bahwa data ini penting untuk kelancaran *project* tertentu.
- **Tenggat Waktu:** Permintaan *deadline* dengan nada yang kooperatif.
