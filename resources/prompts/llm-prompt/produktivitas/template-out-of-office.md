prompt_preview: |-
  You are a professional corporate copywriter.
  I am going on vacation/leave and need an "Out of Office" (OOO) auto-reply email.

  Details:
  - My Date of Return: [INSERT RETURN DATE]
  - Reason (Optional): [INSERT REASON, e.g., Annual Leave / Medical Leave, or leave blank]
  - Urgent Contact Person 1 (Name & Email): [INSERT EMERGENCY CONTACT 1]
  - Urgent Contact Person 2 (Name & Email): [INSERT EMERGENCY CONTACT 2, or leave blank]

  Please draft two variations:
  1. A standard, highly professional corporate OOO reply.
  2. A slightly warmer, friendly OOO reply.
steps:
  - "Salin prompt di atas."
  - "Buka AI favorit Anda."
  - "Isi tanggal kembali Anda, alasan (opsional), dan yang paling penting: siapa rekan kerja yang akan menanggung beban (*backup*) saat Anda pergi beserta emailnya."
  - "Tekan *enter*."
tips:
  - "Memiliki 2 variasi sangat berguna. Jika perusahaan Anda kaku (seperti Bank/Firma Hukum), gunakan opsi 1. Jika di startup atau *creative agency*, opsi 2 akan lebih disukai."

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Dua pilihan pesan otomatis (*auto-responder*):
1. **Versi Formal Korporat:** Kaku, *straight to the point*, langsung memberikan arahan darurat.
2. **Versi Ramah/Kasual:** Lebih hangat, menyapa dengan rileks, namun tetap memberikan informasi kontak darurat secara jelas.
