  Act as a B2B Sales Executive.
  I need to write a professional Proposal/Price Quotation email to a prospective corporate client.

  Details of the Deal:
  - Client Name/Company: [INSERT CLIENT COMPANY]
  - Service/Product Offered: [INSERT WHAT YOU ARE OFFERING]
  - Total Price/Estimate: [INSERT PRICE OR "Attached in the document"]
  - Key Deliverables (What they get): [INSERT 2-3 MAIN DELIVERABLES]

  Please write a formal but persuasive email.
  Structure:
  1. Thank them for the previous discussion/opportunity.
  2. Briefly recap their main problem (to show we listened).
  3. Outline the proposed solution and deliverables clearly.
  4. Mention the pricing and attach the formal document.
  5. Close with a confident Call-to-Action to review and discuss the next steps.

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Email Pengantar *Quotation* Level B2B:
- **Recap Empati:** Mengulang kembali masalah perusahaan klien untuk menunjukkan bahwa Anda adalah pendengar yang baik.
- **Rincian Jelas:** Menjabarkan hasil akhir (Deliverables) yang akan didapat perusahaan klien dengan sangat rapi sebelum masuk ke bagian angka/harga.
