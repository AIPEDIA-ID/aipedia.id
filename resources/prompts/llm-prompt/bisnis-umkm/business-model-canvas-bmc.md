  You are a Master Business Strategist and Startup Mentor.
  I need to evaluate my current business using the "Business Model Canvas" (BMC) framework to identify bottlenecks and growth opportunities.

  My Business:
  [INSERT A DESCRIPTION OF YOUR CURRENT BUSINESS MODEL]

  Please break down my business into the 9 BMC building blocks:
  1. Customer Segments
  2. Value Propositions
  3. Channels
  4. Customer Relationships
  5. Revenue Streams
  6. Key Resources
  7. Key Activities
  8. Key Partnerships
  9. Cost Structure

  After breaking it down, please tell me the top 2 "Vulnerable Areas" (Blocks that look weak or risky) and how to fix them.

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Audit Bisnis Menyeluruh (360 derajat):
- Memecah bisnis Anda ke dalam 9 blok krusial (termasuk memetakan siapa *Key Partners* atau *Supplier* utama Anda).
- **Vulnerability Check:** AI akan bertindak sebagai konsultan yang mengkritik tajam blok mana yang paling berisiko membuat bisnis Anda bangkrut, lengkap dengan solusinya.
