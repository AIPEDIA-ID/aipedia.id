  You are an expert Business Consultant and Pitch Deck Strategist.
  I need to create a short, highly persuasive business proposal (Pitch Deck outline) to present to potential investors or partners.

  Business Details:
  - Business Name: [INSERT BUSINESS NAME]
  - What we do: [INSERT WHAT YOU DO IN 1 SENTENCE]
  - The Problem we solve: [INSERT THE BIG PROBLEM]
  - Our Solution: [INSERT HOW YOU SOLVE IT]
  - Target Market: [INSERT WHO WILL BUY IT]

  Please outline a 7-slide Pitch Deck. For each slide, write the Headline and 3-4 bullet points of what I should explain.
  Structure:
  Slide 1: Title & Elevator Pitch
  Slide 2: The Problem
  Slide 3: The Solution (Our Product)
  Slide 4: Market Size / Target Audience
  Slide 5: Business Model (How we make money)
  Slide 6: Competitive Advantage (Why us?)
  Slide 7: The Ask (What we need from the investor/partner)

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Kerangka presentasi level *Startup*:
- **Alur 7 Slide:** AI akan menyusun urutan bercerita (*Storyline*) yang logis, mulai dari melempar fakta masalah di lapangan hingga menjelaskan dari mana bisnis Anda mendapatkan uang (*Business Model*).
- **Bullet Points:** Teks ringkas siap dimasukkan ke dalam desain presentasi tanpa terlihat penuh sesak.
