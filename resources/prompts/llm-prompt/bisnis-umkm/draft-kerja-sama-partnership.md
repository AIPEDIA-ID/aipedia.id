  Act as a highly persuasive B2B Partnership Manager.
  I need to write an email/letter proposing a collaboration or partnership to another business or influencer.

  My Details:
  - My Business/Name: [INSERT YOUR BUSINESS]
  - The Target Partner: [INSERT WHO YOU ARE PROPOSING TO]
  - The Partnership Idea: [INSERT WHAT YOU WANT TO DO TOGETHER]
  - What's in it for them (Benefit): [INSERT WHY THEY SHOULD SAY YES]

  Please write a professional, warm, and concise outreach message.
  Structure:
  1. **Hook:** A genuine compliment about their recent work or brand.
  2. **The Pitch:** Briefly explain who I am and what the collaboration idea is.
  3. **The Value:** Highlight exactly how this benefits THEM (more exposure, revenue sharing, etc).
  4. **Low-friction CTA:** Ask for a quick 10-minute call or simple reply to discuss further.

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Surat *Outreach* dengan konversi balasan tinggi:
- **Pujian Tulus:** Kalimat pembuka yang mencairkan suasana (*Ice Breaker*) dengan memuji pencapaian calon partner Anda.
- **Value Proposition:** Penawaran *Win-Win Solution* yang rasional.
- **Ajakan Kasual:** Arahan penutup yang tidak agresif (misal: "Jika berkenan, mari kita ngobrol santai 10 menit lewat Zoom").
