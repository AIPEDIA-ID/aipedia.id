  Act as an Operations Manager and Process Engineer.
  I have a messy, unstructured process in my business, and I need to turn it into a clear, step-by-step Standard Operating Procedure (SOP).

  - The Task/Process Name: [INSERT PROCESS NAME, e.g., How to open the cafe every morning]
  - The Raw Steps (Messy thoughts): [INSERT YOUR RAW NOTES/STEPS HERE]

  Please organize my raw notes into a formal SOP document.
  Use this format:
  1. **Purpose:** What is the goal of this SOP?
  2. **Scope:** Who is responsible for doing this?
  3. **Materials Needed:** What tools/items are required?
  4. **Step-by-Step Procedure:** Use clear, numbered lists with action verbs (e.g., "Open the door", "Turn on the machine"). Bold important safety or quality warnings.

Dokumen Standar Operasional yang rapi:
- AI akan "merapikan" ingatan berantakan Anda menjadi dokumen bernomor (*numbered lists*) layaknya panduan manual di pabrik profesional.
- Akan ada kolom peringatan (*Warnings*) agar karyawan tidak melakukan kesalahan fatal di tahap tertentu.
