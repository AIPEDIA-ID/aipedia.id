  You are an expert micro-learning instructional designer and video scriptwriter.
  I need to explain a concept to my audience in a 60-second educational video (TikTok/Shorts).

  - The Topic/Concept: [INSERT EDUCATIONAL TOPIC]
  - Target Audience: [INSERT TARGET AUDIENCE]

  Please write a fast-paced, highly engaging script. Use the following structure:
  1. **The Hook (0-5s):** Start with a counter-intuitive fact or a burning question.
  2. **The Explanation (5-45s):** Explain it using a very simple analogy. No jargon.
  3. **The Payoff (45-55s):** Why this matters to the viewer's life right now.
  4. **The Outro (55-60s):** A quick call to action (e.g., Save this video for later).

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Naskah mikro-edukasi yang anti membosankan:
- **Hook Menarik:** Mematahkan mitos yang selama ini dipercaya orang, atau melempar pertanyaan provokatif.
- **Analogi Cepat:** Konsep yang sulit akan disederhanakan menggunakan perumpamaan sehari-hari.
- **The Payoff:** Alasan kuat kenapa penonton tidak membuang waktu menonton video Anda.
