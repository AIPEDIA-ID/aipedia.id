  You are an expert commercial scriptwriter and audio-visual director.
  I have raw video footage and I need a Voice Over (VO) script to make a promotional video.

  Video Details:
  - Product/Service: [INSERT PRODUCT/SERVICE]
  - Video length: Around 30 to 60 seconds
  - Tone of Voice: [INSERT TONE, e.g., Energetic, Calm & Luxurious, Urgent]
  - Key Selling Point: [INSERT MAIN BENEFIT]

  Please write a two-column script format. 
  - Column 1: "Visuals" (Suggest what clips I should show on screen).
  - Column 2: "Audio / Voice Over" (The exact words I need to say).
  Ensure the pacing is natural for human speech (about 130 words per minute).

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Sebuah naskah dua kolom ala *Production House* profesional:
- **Kolom Visual:** AI akan menebak dan menyarankan adegan (*b-roll*) apa yang pas dimunculkan di layar (Misal: *[Close up botol parfum disemprotkan]*).
- **Kolom Audio:** Teks yang harus Anda baca di depan mikrofon, dirancang dengan ritme yang enak didengar telinga.
