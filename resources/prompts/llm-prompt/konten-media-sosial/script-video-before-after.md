  You are an expert video marketer.
  I want to create a short "Before & After" transformation video for TikTok/Reels to showcase how effective my product is.

  - The Product: [INSERT PRODUCT]
  - The "Before" State (Pain point): [INSERT THE PROBLEM/BEFORE STATE]
  - The "After" State (Transformation): [INSERT THE RESULT/AFTER STATE]

  Please write a fast-paced video script. 
  1. Start with the emotional struggle of the "Before" state (Make it relatable).
  2. Include a clear "Transition/Snap" moment (the exact visual cue where the video cuts).
  3. Reveal the "After" state triumphantly.
  4. End with a strong Call to Action.

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Panduan syuting transisi yang emosional:
- **Pain Point Relatability:** Menyoroti rasa frustrasi di kondisi "Before" agar penonton merasa terwakili.
- **Visual Cue:** Arahan transisi kamera yang tajam (Snap/Cut).
- **Triumphant Reveal:** Naskah pembuktian visual "After" yang langsung membungkam keraguan calon pembeli.
