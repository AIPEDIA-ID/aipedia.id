  You are a viral social media strategist and expert copywriter. 
  I need a script for a short-form video (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts).

  Video Parameters:
  - Topic/Product: [INSERT TOPIC OR PRODUCT]
  - Target Audience: [INSERT TARGET AUDIENCE]
  - Desired length: Under 60 seconds

  Please write a compelling script using the following structure:
  1. **The 3-Second Hook:** Provide 3 different extremely catchy opening hooks (choose from: curiosity gap, bold statement, or relatable pain point).
  2. **The Body (Value):** Keep it punchy, fast-paced, and easy to read on a teleprompter.
  3. **Call to Action (CTA):** A clear instruction on what the viewer should do next.
  4. **Visual Cues:** Include bracketed notes [like this] for text on screen, B-roll ideas, or sound effects.

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Naskah lengkap layaknya sutradara profesional:
- **Opsi Hook:** 3 pilihan kalimat pertama (Contoh: "Jangan beli produk A sebelum kamu nonton video ini!").
- **Body & Visual Cues:** Dialog utama yang diselingi instruksi kapan Anda harus menampilkan teks di layar (*Pop-up Text*) atau kapan harus mengganti *angle* kamera.
- **CTA:** Arahan jelas di akhir video (Misal: *Klik link di bio* atau *Komen untuk part 2*).
