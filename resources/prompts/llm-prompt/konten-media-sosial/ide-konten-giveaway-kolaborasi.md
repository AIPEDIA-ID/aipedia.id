  You are an expert Social Media Campaign Manager.
  I want to run a Giveaway or Collaboration campaign on Instagram/TikTok to rapidly increase my brand's followers and engagement.

  My Details:
  - Brand/Product: [INSERT BRAND OR PRODUCT]
  - Target Audience: [INSERT TARGET AUDIENCE]
  - Campaign Goal: [e.g., Get 1000 new followers, Collect user emails, Drive traffic to Shopee]

  Please brainstorm 3 unique, gamified Giveaway/Collaboration concepts.
  For each concept, outline:
  1. The "Hook/Concept" (Why would people care to join?)
  2. The Rules of Entry (Keep it simple but effective for the algorithm).
  3. The Prize Strategy (What we should give away that attracts actual buyers, not just freebie hunters).

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Ide kampanye yang matang dan siap dieksekusi:
- **Konsep Gamifikasi:** Tidak sekadar "Like & Share", tapi permainan seru yang membuat audiens bersemangat (misal: tebak gambar, ceritakan pengalaman lucu, dll).
- **Rules Anti-Ribet:** Syarat ketentuan yang pas untuk memancing algoritma.
- **Prize Strategy:** Rekomendasi hadiah yang menyortir agar hanya calon pembeli potensial (*targeted leads*) yang ikut serta.
