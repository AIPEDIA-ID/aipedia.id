prompt_preview: |-
  Act as a rigorous and critical university examiner for my upcoming thesis defense / oral exam.
  My topic/presentation is about: [INSERT THESIS TITLE OR TOPIC]
  Here is a brief summary/abstract of my work: [INSERT ABSTRACT OR SUMMARY]

  We will do a simulation. 
  Rules:
  1. Ask me ONE challenging, critical question at a time. 
  2. Wait for my answer. 
  3. After I answer, critique my response (was it logical? did it lack evidence?) and then ask your next question.
  4. Keep the tone formal, probing, and intellectually demanding.

  Start the simulation by introducing yourself as the examiner and asking your first question.

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Sebuah **Sesi Roleplay (Simulasi Interaktif)**:
- AI tidak akan langsung memuntahkan semua daftar pertanyaan, melainkan bertanya **satu per satu**.
- Setiap kali Anda mengetik/berbicara jawaban Anda, AI akan mengevaluasi apakah jawaban Anda masuk akal, lalu membalas dengan pertanyaan lanjutan (follow-up question) yang lebih menyudutkan.
