prompt_preview: |-
  You are an expert Customer Support Specialist.
  I received a complaint from an angry customer on WhatsApp/Email, and I need a professional, empathetic response to calm them down and resolve the issue.

  The Situation:
  - Customer Name: [INSERT CUSTOMER NAME]
  - The Complaint: [INSERT WHAT THEY ARE ANGRY ABOUT, e.g., Item arrived broken, Late shipping]
  - The Solution I can offer: [INSERT YOUR SOLUTION, e.g., Send a replacement, Give a 20% refund]

  Please write a polite and de-escalating response message.
  Structure:
  1. **Apologize & Validate:** Sincerely apologize and validate their frustration without making excuses.
  2. **The Explanation (Brief):** A very brief explanation of what went wrong (do not blame the customer).
  3. **The Solution:** Clearly state the solution I am offering.
  4. **Next Steps:** Tell them exactly what will happen next and how to contact us if they need more help.
steps:
  - "Salin teks prompt di atas."
  - "Buka ChatGPT atau Claude (Claude lebih unggul dalam bahasa empati)."
  - "Sebutkan nama pembeli, apa akar masalahnya (misal: *paket nyasar*), dan solusi damai apa yang sanggup Anda berikan."
  - "Tekan *enter*."
tips:
  - "Hal terburuk saat menghadapi komplain adalah bersikap defensif atau 'membela diri'. AI akan merangkai kata-kata yang memvalidasi rasa frustrasi pelanggan (*I hear you*) sebelum menawarkan solusi."
---

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Teks De-eskalasi (Peredam Amarah):
- **Bahasa Empati:** Permintaan maaf tulus yang tidak terdengar seperti jawaban robot (Contoh: "Halo Kak Budi, saya sangat mengerti kenapa Kakak kecewa...").
- **Solusi Tegas:** Langsung menuju titik penyelesaian masalah tanpa memaksa pelanggan berdebat panjang.
