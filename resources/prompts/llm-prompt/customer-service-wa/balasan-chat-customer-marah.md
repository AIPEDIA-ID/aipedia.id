prompt_preview: |-
  You are an expert in Conflict Resolution and Crisis De-escalation (similar to an FBI hostage negotiator).
  I have a customer who is furiously angry in my chat, using aggressive language, and threatening to leave bad reviews or go viral.

  - The Customer's Core Grievance: [INSERT WHY THEY ARE FURIOUS]
  - My Stance / Solution: [INSERT WHAT I CAN AND CANNOT DO]

  Please write a response to de-escalate this crisis immediately.
  Use the "HEAR" framework:
  1. **Hear:** Acknowledge their anger without being defensive.
  2. **Empathize:** Show that if I were in their shoes, I would be mad too.
  3. **Analyze:** State the facts of the situation calmly.
  4. **Resolve:** Offer the absolute best solution I can provide right now.

  The tone must be incredibly calm, professional, and neutralizing.

  - "Jelaskan dengan jujur kenapa pembeli tersebut mengamuk, dan sebutkan apa batas maksimal ganti rugi yang bisa Anda berikan."
  - "Tekan *enter*."

- Menggunakan taktik psikologis (Framework HEAR) untuk memvalidasi emosi kemarahan mereka di awal kalimat, yang secara instan akan menurunkan tensi pembeli sebelum mereka membaca solusi Anda.
