  Act as a Customer Loyalty Expert.
  I need a warm, memorable "Thank You" message to send to customers after they receive their order. 

  Details:
  - Product/Brand Name: [INSERT BRAND OR PRODUCT]
  - Target Emotion: [e.g., Grateful, Excited for them, Professional]
  - Soft Ask (Optional): [e.g., Ask for a photo review, Ask them to tag us on Instagram]

  Please write a short Post-Purchase message.
  Structure:
  1. **The Gratitude:** A sincere thank you that highlights how much their support means to our small business.
  2. **The Check-in:** Ask if the product arrived safely and if they are happy with it.
  3. **The Soft Ask:** Gently ask for a review/tag without being demanding.
  4. **The Sign-off:** A warm closing from a real person (e.g., "Warmly, [My Name] from [Brand]").

  - "Tekan *enter*."
tips:
  - "Biaya mencari pelanggan baru jauh lebih mahal daripada mempertahankan pelanggan lama. Ucapan terima kasih yang tulus akan langsung membedakan level pelayanan *Brand* Anda dari toko-toko pesaing."

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Teks Perawat Loyalitas (*Retention*):
- Sentuhan personal yang menyadarkan pembeli bahwa mereka sedang bertransaksi dengan manusia yang berterima kasih atas uang mereka, bukan sekadar mesin korporat.
- Membantu meningkatkan jumlah *Review Bintang 5* secara organik.
