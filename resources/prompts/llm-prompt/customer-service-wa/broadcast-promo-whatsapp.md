prompt_preview: |-
  Act as an expert WhatsApp Marketer.
  I need to send a Broadcast Message to my past customers to announce a new promo/product, but I DO NOT want it to sound like an annoying spam message.

  Details:
  - Product/Promo: [INSERT PROMO/PRODUCT]
  - Target Audience: [INSERT WHO YOU ARE MESSAGING, e.g., Previous buyers of X]
  - The Offer: [INSERT DISCOUNT OR OFFER]

  Please write a conversational, low-pressure broadcast message.
  Structure:
  1. **The Friendly Opener:** Greet them like a human, not a robot (e.g., "Halo kak, semoga kabarnya baik!").
  2. **The "Reason Why":** Give a legitimate reason why you are messaging them today (e.g., "Karena kakak dulu pernah beli...").
  3. **The Soft Pitch:** Present the promo/product without being pushy.
  4. **The "Opt-out" / CTA:** Give them the link to buy, but also include a polite note saying they can ignore the message if they aren't interested.

  - "Salin teks prompt di atas."

  - "Tekan *enter*."
tips:

# Output yang Diharapkan dari AI:

Teks *Broadcast* yang Sopan dan Mengundang Klik:
- **Alasan yang Kuat:** AI akan membuat alasan logis kenapa Anda mengirim pesan tersebut, sehingga pelanggan merasa spesial (*Exclusive Offer*).
- **Format Anti-Blokir:** Pesan yang terkesan ditulis manual, bukan hasil *copy-paste* robot promosi.
